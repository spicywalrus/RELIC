# Features

**Home**: banner art and tagline for the week's theme (52 themes, 6 animal emblems), Draw New Week, This Week's Outlook (Sunday to Saturday, today on a plum shield, tap days to cycle dot marks), Ledger (top three tasks, tap to open the list), Reliquary link with bell jar, trio of flip cards (Pilgrimage, Curious Encounter, Malady) with Complete (+gold) and one reroll per week, hourglass that drains over the week and flips on a new week, health and gold badge.

**Sidebar** (icons with labels): Home, Daily, Hearth, Mood Mountain, Egg Thief, Life Force, Reliquary, Gold Bank, Wishlist, Deck, Skill Tree, Dreamcatcher, Archive, Reflection. The top medallion collapses it like a drawer.

**Ledger list**: add, edit, check off, delete (with Recently deleted and Restore), star up to three, Easy/Medium/Hard (10/20/30 gold), optional red skull (urgent, 10 damage if missed) or blue skull (important, 1 damage per day), sorted starred, red, blue, gold.

**Daily**: editable daily rituals with check circles, 10 gold once all are done, sidebar icon becomes a check.

**Hearth**: chores with icons, weekday chips (auto-add to the Ledger that day), Alex/Helen toggle (Helen's chores skip your ledger and gold), 5 gold default, add or remove activities.

**Mood Mountain**: 1 to 10 blue-to-red check-in with optional reason, 30-day mountain chart, monthly peak and valley.

**Egg Thief**: songs with five stages (Ideation, Demo, Mix, Master, Release), egg marker, audio version player (file, drag, paste or link).

**Life Force**: heart that refreshes to 100 each month and takes skull damage.

**Reliquary**: weekly blocks with photo collage, notes, audio or music links, purchased rewards.

**Gold Bank**: week and total counters (tap to edit), rewards shop (three random rewards per week with icons), monthly gold bar with goal line and mystery prize from the Wishlist, clear buttons.

**Wishlist**, **Deck** (browse and add themes and cards, tiers, Use this week), **Skill Tree** (levels 1 to 5, monthly focus with one reshuffle), **Dreamcatcher** (tagged ideas, send to Ledger).

**Archive**: 52 week squares per year with theme icons, year arrows, tap for a weekly snapshot.

**Reflection**: five questions per week from a pool of 22, song or album of the week, closes the week once three are answered. On Sunday from 5am the star pulses; after reflecting, a flashing button reviews the finished week and loads the next.
